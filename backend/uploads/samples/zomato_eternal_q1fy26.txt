Retail Equity Research - Eternal Ltd. (Formerly Zomato Ltd.)
Q1FY26 Result Update | Date: 29th July, 2025

Company Overview:
Eternal Limited operates as an online food delivery and quick commerce company. It runs a B2C platform under the Zomato brand. The company also operates Hyperpure and Blinkit.

Key Financial Performance:
- Consolidated revenue from operations surged 70.4% YoY in Q1FY26 to Rs. 7,167cr as all segments demonstrated robust growth.
- Revenue from the quick commerce business (Blinkit) soared 154.8% YoY to Rs. 2,400cr.
- Net order value (NOV) of B2C businesses rose 55% YoY to Rs. 20,183cr in Q1FY26.
- EBITDA fell 35.0% YoY to Rs. 115cr in Q1FY26 due to higher operating expenses; EBITDA margin declined to 1.6%.
- Reported PAT stood at Rs. 25cr in Q1FY26.
- Blinkit added 243 new stores in Q1FY26, taking total stores to 1,544.
