Executive Summary:
Apple Inc. reported quarterly revenue of $85.8 billion, up 5% year-over-year. Services revenue reached an all-time record of $24.2 billion, up 14% YoY driven by expanding active installed base crossing 2.2 billion active devices globally.

Key Highlights:
- iPhone revenue reached $39.3 billion with strong adoption in emerging markets.
- Mac revenue grew 2% YoY to $7.0 billion backed by M3 Silicon upgrades.
- Services gross margin expanded to 74.0%, boosting consolidated EBITDA margin to 32.5%.
- Operating cash flow totaled $28.9 billion, enabling $32 billion returned to shareholders via dividends and share buybacks.
- Apple Intelligence features rollout slated for Q4 FY25 expected to drive supercycle upgrades.
